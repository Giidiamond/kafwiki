others, made this a into disgusting flesh hell.

Current state

As said before, the Papergutory is currently a flesh hell, or, to say the very least, a mostly unlivable environment for anyone who weren't affected by them.
The non-unlivable places are the only salvations there are in the Papergutory. The only downside of these safe spaces is they are underground or underwater,
depending on if you are on land or on water. They got turned into a fleshly, violent vessel or a monstrous version of themselves, or they either were able
